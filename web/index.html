If you still disagree after asking

Ask for the job card and the old parts before paying. If a question isn't resolved, the National Consumer Helpline (1915, or consumerhelpline.gov.in) can advise. This is general guidance, not legal advice.

Open the manufacturer's recall lookup?

Only the manufacturer can confirm whether a recall applies to your vehicle. If you enter your VIN or chassis number on their page, it is shared with them. FairFix doesn't send it anywhere.
